Privacy

We collect only what is needed to connect donors and people in need: name, age, blood group, city and mobile number, plus request details.

The public pages show only totals by blood group and city. Phone numbers are visible to the BloodBridge team, and a requester’s number may be shared with a matching donor.

We do not sell or advertise with your data. To be deleted, {% if CONTACT %}email {{ CONTACT }}{% else %}contact the site owner{% endif %} and we will remove your record.

BloodBridge is a volunteer project, not a medical service. Eligibility and safety are decided by a licensed blood bank or doctor.
